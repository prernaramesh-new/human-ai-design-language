Follow the evidence

See the boundary

Show what the AI can access, what it has actually read, and what remains off limits.

Solution placeholder

Give clear permission

Before asking for access, make the task, scope, and time limit clear.

Solution placeholder

See what will change

Before asking for approval, show what the action will affect.

Solution placeholder

See what changed

After an action, show everything it changed, including anything it took out.

Solution placeholder

Keep a way back

Let people stop further work and reverse changes where possible. Be clear about what cannot be undone.
